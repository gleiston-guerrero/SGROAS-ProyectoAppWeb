# Informed Consent Form — SUS Usability Evaluation

## Project
SGROAS — Fleet Transport Management System
Universidad Técnica Estatal de Quevedo (UTEQ)

## Purpose
You are invited to participate in a usability evaluation of the SGROAS web application.
The System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire will be administered after you complete
a set of predefined tasks on the system.

## Procedure
1. You will perform 5 tasks on the SGROAS web application.
2. After completing the tasks, you will answer the 10-item SUS questionnaire.
3. The entire session will take approximately 15-20 minutes.

## Voluntary Participation
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without
penalty or consequence.

## Confidentiality
All responses are anonymous. Your identity will be replaced with a code (P01-P15).
No personal data will be published. The raw data will be stored securely and
used only for academic purposes.

## Data Protection
In accordance with the Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales (LOPDP),
your data will be treated with strict confidentiality and used solely for the
purposes described in this consent form.
